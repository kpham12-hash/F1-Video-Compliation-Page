Formula 1

Formula 1 is the most elite class of motorsport in the world. Every year, they attract billions and billions of fans and dollars. Formula 1 has a total of 11 F1 teams on the grid starting in 2026 and beyond. The majority of them are car manufacturers, while the minorities are smaller or big F1 Teams that are each owned by various companies from finance, energy drinks, legacy F1 Teams, and more. We're gonna be showing you at least 3 on this web page. So buck up, and enjoy the show!

Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula 1 Team

The Mercedes-AMG Petronas Formula 1 Team is the greatest F1 team in the 21st century that has achieved more race wins than any other F1 team on the grid with its powerful, dominant engines from the PU106A Hybrid (2014) that basically launched them into the beginning of their championship streak. Then later in the next few season they launched the PU106B Hybrid (2015–2016), which propelled them to continue their championship streak. They also had other dominant engines from M08 EQ Power+, M09 EQ Power+, M10 EQ Power+, and M11/M12 EQ. Those engines were the late evolutions of the hybrid power unit that helped them secure a record of 8 constructors (team) championships.

Audi Revolut F1 Team

The Audi Revolut F1 Team is one of the newest Formula Teams to join the grid following the withdrawal of the Sauber F1 Team. In early of 2023, Audi bought a minority stake in the Sauber F1 team, then they sped up plans to buy ownership of Sauber Holding AG. In November of 2024, they sold a minority stake to the QATAR Investment Authority. Around January 2025, they finalized full 100% ownership of Sauber Holding AG. In Late December of 2025, the team officially rebranded as the Audi Revolut F1 Team ahead of its 2026 race debut.

Today: At the team's Berlin launch in January 2026, Audi said it had begun a five-year plan aimed at challenging for championships by 2030. Audi debuted at the season-opening Australian Grand Prix in 2026 with the R26, designed for new chassis and power unit regulations. Gabriel Bortoleto and Nico Hülkenberg qualified 10th and 11th, respectively. Bortoleto finished the race in 9th place, scoring Audi's first-ever points in Formula One, while Hülkenberg was unable to take the start due to technical issues.

Atlassian Williams Formula 1 Team

The Atlassian Williams F1 Team is one of the oldest F1 teams to be on the grid in the 21st century. It was founded by Frank Williams and Patrick Head in the year of 1977. It was originally known as Williams Grand Prix Engineering Limited. Then in the 1980s and 1990s, it quickly rose through the rankings and standings to become a powerhouse with engine partnerships from Honda and Renault. The legendary Adrian Newey helped contribute the work of designing the most capable and advanced cars during that era to produce 9 contructors championships and 7 drivers championships with legendary drivers like Nigel Mansell and Alain Prost. In the golden era, it was shortly followed by the death of Ayrton Senna in the year 1994. Which shortly followed by a long financial and competitive decline in the 2000s and 2010s. Then the Williams Family sold the historic outfit to an American investment firm called Dorilton Capital in 2020. Today, the team is undergoing a modern revival under Team Principal James Vowles, steadily upgrading its infrastructure to climb back to the front of the grid.
